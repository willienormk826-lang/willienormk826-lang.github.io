Privacy Policy — DAJG Payslip

Effective date: July 2026

Who we are

The DAJG Payslip app is provided by the Human Resources / Payroll office of the Department of Justice & Attorney General (DAJG), Papua New Guinea, so that staff can view and download their own payslips on their phones.

What information the app handles

The app displays payslip documents that the Department's payroll office has already prepared for you. Payslips contain your name, file number, pay, allowances, deductions, and bank disbursement details.

What the app collects

The app does not collect analytics, does not track you, does not use advertising, and does not send any of your information to third parties. Your file number and (if you set one) your changed password are stored only on your own phone, so that you can sign in. Payslips you view are saved on your phone so they remain available offline.

Sign-in

Access requires your staff file number and password. Each staff member can only open their own payslips.

Data sharing

No personal data is shared with anyone. If you use the app's Share button, you choose where your own payslip is sent.

Data removal

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone settings, removes all locally saved data including saved payslips and your password.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your payslips, contact the DAJG Payroll/HR office.
